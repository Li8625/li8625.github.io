## 10 Interesting Facts About Otters

1. Otters are members of the weasel family, Mustelidae.

2. There are 13 commonly recognized species of otters worldwide.

3. Sea otters have exceptionally dense fur that helps keep them warm in cold water.

4. Sea otters sometimes hold paws while resting in the water.

5. Some sea otters use rocks to crack open shellfish.

6. Baby otters are called pups.

7. Sea otters often float on their backs while eating.

8. Giant otters can grow to approximately 6 feet long, including their tails.

9. Otters communicate through various sounds, including whistles, chirps, and growls.

10. Sea otters are important to kelp forest ecosystems because they eat sea urchins.

---

## Bonus Otter Fact

Unlike seals and whales, sea otters rely on their thick fur rather than a layer of blubber to stay warm.
